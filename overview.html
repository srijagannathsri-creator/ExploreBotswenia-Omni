🌐 Introduction

The Federal Republic of Botswenia is a sovereign nation located in Asia, in the Indian Ocean region. With a population of approximately 449.82 million people and a total geographical area of around 2.13 million square kilometres with GDP of 3.2 Trillion USD. Botswenia is a country of considerable geographical, cultural and economic diversity. Its capital city, Kworsau, serves as the national centre of administration and governance. The nation is characterised by its rich cultural heritage, multilingual society, extensive transport networks and distinctive national identity. Guided by the principles of Knowledge, Unity and Progress, Botswenia continues to develop across various sectors while maintaining its national traditions and institutions.

🏙️ Capital City – Kworsau

Kworsau is the capital city of the Federal Republic of Botswenia. it falls under Kworsau Capital District and its the national capital territory. It serves as the country's principal centre of national administration, governance and public institutions. As the seat of the national government, Kworsau plays an important role in the political and administrative affairs of Botswenia. The city is also home to Kworsau International Airport (KWX), connecting the capital with domestic and international destinations.

🇧🇼 National Flag-ស៉ាំ អិ រ៉ូ តូកែវា

The National Flag of the Federal Republic of Botswenia called ស៉ាំ អិ រ៉ូ តូកែវា in vuluz is a symbol of the nation's identity, unity, values and aspirations. It consists of three horizontal bands of green, white and red, with three yellow seven-pointed stars arranged horizontally across the central white band.

📜 Historical Background

  • First Proposed By: Vesukaba Nigatoke
  • Date of First Proposal: 09 July 1951
  • Official Adoption Date: 12 December 1953
  • Aspect Ratio: 3:2

🎨 Meaning of the Colours

1. Green Band – Agriculture and Nature

  • Agriculture and fertility
  • Nature and growth
  • Forests, valleys and national prosperity

2. White Band – Peace and Unity

  • Peace and mutual brotherhood
  • Unity among all states
  • Justice, fairness and constitutional purity

3. Red Band – Courage and Strength

  • Courage and sacrifice
  • Energy and strength
  • Confidence and determination

⭐ The Three National Stars

The three yellow seven-pointed stars represent 21 national values, with each star symbolising a distinct group of seven values.

1. Left Star – Knowledge and Progress

  1. Knowledge
  2. Wisdom
  3. Progress
  4. Innovation
  5. Truth
  6. Curiosity
  7. Enlightenment

2. Middle Star – Unity and Understanding

  1. Unity
  2. Harmony
  3. Justice
  4. Peace
  5. Brotherhood
  6. Tolerance
  7. Equality

3. Right Star – Courage and Strength

  1. Courage
  2. Strength
  3. Resilience
  4. Hope
  5. Sacrifice
  6. Dignity
  7. Perseverance

National Emblem

Official Name

និមិត្តសញ្ញារដ្ឋ វ៉ា បោត្សុវេនិអ

Historical Background

The National State Emblem of the Federal Republic of Botswenia was proposed by Jandong Bahsey on 13 April 1955. Following its proposal, it was officially adopted on 15 January 1956, becoming an important national symbol representing the country's identity and sovereignty.

National Motto

The national motto of Botswenia is "Knowledge, Unity, Progress." In Vuluz, it is written as "ចំណេះដឹង ឯកភាព វឌ្ឍនភាព". The motto represents the fundamental values of knowledge, national unity and continuous progress that guide the nation.
